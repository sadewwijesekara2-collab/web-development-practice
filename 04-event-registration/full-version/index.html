About the Event

TechNova 2026 is a one-day technology festival designed to bring students together to learn, create and share ideas. On Saturday, 5 December 2026, the University Main Hall will host coding challenges, practical workshops, project exhibitions and talks from people working in the industry. Whether you are just starting out or already building your own projects, you can pick up new skills, get feedback on your work and meet others who share your interests. Attending is a great way to gain confidence, discover possible career paths and turn curiosity about technology into real experience.

What's Happening

Event Activities

Coding Challenge

Featured Activity

Test your problem-solving skills in a friendly, beginner-welcoming coding competition. Work through a series of short puzzles at your own pace, either alone or with a partner. No experience is needed, and everyone who takes part will learn something new.

Project Exhibition

See what students have built in a showcase of creative technology projects. Presenters will demonstrate their apps, websites and prototypes and explain how they made them. Visitors can ask questions, give feedback and pick up ideas for their own projects.

Web Development Workshop

Join a practical, hands-on session where you build a simple web page from scratch. You will learn how HTML and CSS work together by writing and testing code step by step. Bring a laptop and leave with a small project you can keep improving.

Technology Career Talk

Hear from professionals who work in the technology industry about their journeys and daily work. They will share advice on skills to learn, how to find opportunities and how to get started in a tech career. There will be time for questions at the end.
