Information for Technology Workshop 2026

Event Location

The Technology Workshop 2026 will be held at the Richmond College Premises, providing a spacious and well-equipped environment for all sessions — from coding labs to the robotics workshop and project exhibition area. The venue is easily accessible and offers ample space for participants, exhibitors, and guest speakers throughout the day. Signboards and volunteers will be available on-site to help guide attendees to their respective session halls.

Date & Time

The Technology Workshop 2026 will be held on Saturday, December 5, 2026 from 09:00 AM to 06:00 PM.

Chief Guest

This session will be conducted by our honorable chief guest, Eng. Prof. Shantha Fernando, Head of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, University of Moratuwa. He holds a PhD from TU Delft, an MPhil from the University of Moratuwa, and a BSc Engineering (Hons) also from Moratuwa, bringing decades of academic and industry experience to the discussion.

His presence adds immense value to the Technology Career Discussion session, offering students a rare opportunity to gain insights directly from one of Sri Lanka's leading figures in computer science and engineering education.

What You Should Bring
  • Notebook and Pens
  • Water Bottle
  • Laptop or Tablet (Note: The organizers are not responsible for any loss or damage to personal devices)
  • Lunch
Event Rules and Regulations
  • Participants must arrive on time and complete registration before 9:00 AM.
  • School uniform is mandatory for all attendees.
  • Maintain discipline and follow instructions given by organizers and session instructors.
  • Personal laptops, if brought, are the responsibility of the participant — organizers are not liable for any loss or damage.
  • Mobile phones should be kept on silent mode during sessions.
  • Participants must stay within designated session areas unless permitted otherwise.
  • Please do not throw rubbish within the school premises — use the designated bins provided.
  • Any form of misconduct or disruptive behavior may lead to removal from the event.
