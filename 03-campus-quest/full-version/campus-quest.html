About the Event

Campus Quest is a fun team challenge where students explore the campus, solve clues, and race to complete each checkpoint. It's a great way to meet new people, discover places you may not have visited before, and build teamwork and problem-solving skills. Whether you're a first-year student or a final-year veteran, everyone is welcome to join. Form your team, register today, and get ready for an exciting adventure!
